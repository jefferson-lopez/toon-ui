# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, safe, and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent, but communicate clearly.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and design decisions, not people.
- Welcome questions from learners and contributors.
- Prefer evidence and technical reasoning over ego.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Public or private intimidation
- Publishing private information without consent
- Repeated bad-faith arguments that block collaboration

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the security/contact channel you publish for the project.
